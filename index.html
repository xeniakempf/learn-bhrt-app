Frequently asked questions

Is this CME-accredited?

No — this is an independent educational course and clinical reference tool, not an accredited CME activity. It's designed to build practical, working knowledge, not to satisfy a CME requirement.

Do I need a special license or DEA registration to use this?

The content is written for licensed prescribers (MD/DO, NP, PA). It doesn't grant any prescribing authority — your own license and state scope of practice govern what you're able to prescribe.

What happens after my year of app access ends?

Your access simply pauses — nothing is deleted. Renew anytime for $149/year to pick back up where you left off.

Can I upgrade from App Only to the full course later?

Yes — reach out and we'll credit what you've already paid toward the Complete Course price.

Is this medical advice?

No. This is educational content for licensed clinicians and does not replace clinical judgment, informed consent, or shared decision-making with your own patients.
